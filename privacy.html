Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Glowdive is made by Samvel Gevorgyan. This policy explains what the game stores.

Offline play

Progress (pearls, divers, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not collect it.

Online play

Online co-op uses Unity Gaming Services. When you play online the game creates an anonymous player ID and temporary room data so players can connect. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or location. You can delete this ID at any time in Settings > Delete my online data.

Ads

Glowdive shows optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area and UK we first ask for your consent. On iOS we ask permission (App Tracking Transparency) before AdMob may use your advertising identifier (IDFA); if you decline, ads are not personalised. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics; see Google's Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data. We do not receive this data ourselves. You can buy Remove Ads to stop ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. Purchase status is stored on your device and restored through Apple.

Children

Glowdive does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
